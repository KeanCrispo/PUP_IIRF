Data Privacy Notice

In accordance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173), the Polytechnic University of the Philippines – Mulanay Branch ensures the protection of your personal and sensitive information.

Purpose of Data Collection

The information collected through the Individual Inventory Record Form (IIRF) will be used solely for the following purposes:

  • Student profiling and guidance services
  • Academic and career counseling
  • Psychological and socio-emotional support
  • Educational research and planning
  • Student welfare and development programs

Type of Information Collected

  • Personal Information: Name, birthdate, address, contact details, etc.
  • Educational Background: Academic performance, school attended, honors received
  • Family Background: Parent/guardian information, family income, number of siblings
  • Health and Personal Circumstances: Medical conditions, social involvement, interests, and personal challenges
  • Other relevant data to provide holistic student support

Data Protection

Your data is stored securely and only authorized personnel (e.g., guidance counselors, program heads) will have access for legitimate educational purposes. Strict physical, organizational, and technical safeguards are in place to ensure confidentiality.

Data Sharing

We do not share your data with third parties without your consent, except as required by law or in cases of emergency affecting your welfare.

Data Retention

Your information will be retained only as long as necessary for the stated purposes and will be securely disposed of afterward, in compliance with the University’s retention policy.

Your Rights

Under the Data Privacy Act, you have the right to:

  • Be informed about the collection and use of your data
  • Access, correct, or delete your personal information
  • Object to processing under certain conditions
  • Lodge a complaint with the National Privacy Commission if your data privacy rights are violated
